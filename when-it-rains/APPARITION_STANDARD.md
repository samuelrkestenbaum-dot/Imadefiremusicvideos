# APPARITION LEGIBILITY STANDARD
### The law for every "her" sighting — built and unbuilt.
*(Written after the six-lens director review: five reviewers independently
flagged five different ghost beats as "doesn't read." That is not five
problems — it is one system problem. Every apparition, from the locked intro
to the unbuilt bridge, is graded against this standard before it ships.)*

---

## Why this exists
The engine of the film is the chain of almost-sightings. If the audience does
not register "that was her / that could be her" on a six-inch phone screen in
the ~1–2 seconds the beat lasts, the scene collapses into "a man in the rain,"
and the story stops moving. Beauty is not the bar. **Legibility is the bar.**

## The five requirements (ALL must pass)
1. **EDGE CONTRAST.** The figure must separate from its background by luminance
   OR color, not just by being "there." A dark smear on a dark window fails.
   Target: the figure's silhouette edge is visibly distinct at 480p downscale
   (test: shrink the frame to 480px wide — can you still find her? If no, fail).
2. **ONE IDENTIFYING CUE.** Exactly one unambiguous "her" signal, held long
   enough to read: her **long dark wavy hair mass** (primary cue, works best),
   OR a consistent **coat/color silhouette** we've trained the audience on.
   Never her face (bible). One cue, not zero, not three.
3. **MINIMUM HOLD 1.5s.** The figure must be legible for at least 1.5 continuous
   seconds before it dissolves/ripples/passes. Blink-and-gone (<1s) reads as an
   artifact, not an apparition. At 24fps that is ≥36 frames of clear presence.
4. **A CLEAN DISSOLVE.** She must LEAVE by a motivated mechanism the audience
   sees — a raindrop hits the puddle, the glass fogs, she passes behind a
   pillar, the reflection trembles apart. Never a hard cut (reads as an edit,
   not a vanishing). The dissolve is the emotional beat; give it its own ~0.5s.
5. **THE SEES-CHART IS OBEYED** (below). Whether HE sees her or only WE do is a
   story fact per rung — never left to chance in the take.

## The he-sees / we-sees chart (per ladder rung)
The ladder only works if his awareness escalates on schedule. Getting this
wrong (him seeing her too early) collapses the build.

| Rung | Section | HE sees? | WE see? | The point of the beat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | INTRO/V1 | no | no (objects only) | absence lives in objects (pillow, 2nd mug) |
| 1 | INTRO window (locked) | **half** — a flicker he can't trust | yes, briefly | first doubt planted |
| 2 | V2 shoulder-look | no — he LOOKS, sees nothing | **audience-only** puddle | the searching begins; WE catch what he misses |
| 3 | PRE1 (delivered) | no — signs, not her | no (world echoes: trees, breath) | dread rises without a sighting |
| 4 | CH1 cafe (locked) | **YES — first true sighting** | yes | he can no longer pretend it's nothing |
| 5 | V3/V4 | he searches on purpose; finds her THINGS | reflection in the turned mirror = only him | confronting, not catching; evidence she was real |
| 6 | PRE2 | he follows a figure — WRONG face (stranger) | yes, then the reveal it's not-her | the chase peaks and FAILS |
| 7 | CH2 | everywhere at once | yes, every surface, fast | full haunting; unsustainable |
| 8 | BRIDGE | he STOPS looking | reflections become ONLY him | the ghost was the chase |
| 9 | FINAL | he looks on purpose — just glass | just glass, just rain | acceptance without closure |

**Reading the chart:** rung 2 (V2 puddle) and rung 5 (V3/V4 mirror) are
**audience-only** — he must NOT react to her, or the rung-4 cafe "first true
sighting" loses its status. This is why the V2 puddle was made audience-only in
the quality pass (he turns a beat late, ripples already settling).

## Grading procedure (every apparition, before ship)
1. Build the beat to the five requirements.
2. Frame-QC at delivery resolution AND at 480p downscale (the phone test).
3. State the rung and confirm the sees-chart row is what the take actually shows.
4. If it fails any requirement, it does not ship — re-derive, don't nudge.

## Retro-grade of what's already built (honest audit)
- **INTRO window (rung 1, locked):** the smear reads as edit-grammar more than
  apparition (reviewer flag). Inside intro_v6 which the user locked — fix would
  reopen the lock. FLAG for the pre-FINAL polish, not now.
- **V1 window ghost (rung 0.5):** faint figure behind glass. Passes hold, weak
  on edge contrast — acceptable as "barely-there" by design, but re-grade if the
  V1 kitchen is ever re-rendered.
- **V2 puddle (rung 2, delivered):** audience-only reflection, rippled apart by
  a drop. This is the standard done right — use as the reference implementation.
- **CH1 cafe reveal (rung 4, locked):** the pastry-case reflection — reviewers
  called it the freshest shot in the film. Reference for rung 4+.
