# WHEN IT RAINS — Director's Treatment (v1, for reaction)

**Logline.** A man haunted by a love he's lost finds her everywhere in a rainstorm.
The rain *is* her. Across the song he moves from tenderly feeling her → searching →
denying her → breaking down and surrendering to the flood → left alone in the last
drops, still wondering.

**The rule that makes it non-random:** every image is triggered by the line being
sung, and every cut lands on a downbeat (61.5 BPM grid, already mapped). The song
drives the picture, not the other way around.

## Two worlds (so the cutting means something)
- **PRESENT** — the man, now, alone. Cold blue-grey storm light, 35mm grain,
  anamorphic, rain on every window. This is where he sings (lip-sync).
- **MEMORY** — her. Warm amber, soft/overexposed, fragmentary. She is **never fully
  present** — a hand, a turn, a reflection, a shape in the cloud. (Serves the grief,
  and hides AI face-consistency drift by design.)
- **THE CHORUS** — the two worlds collide *in the water itself*: she appears in the
  rain, the puddles, the lightning. Full band performance + storm peak.

## Casting / consistency
- **Lead** = our established man (short red-blonde hair, receding hairline, reddish
  beard; anchor still `5cc8239a`). He lip-syncs the hook and each verse's opening line.
- **The woman** = a NEW consistent, reference-anchored memory character. Glimpsed only.

## Look / cutting rhythm
- Slow tempo → verses breathe (3–5s cuts); choruses & bridge get denser and faster.
- Every cut on a downbeat. **No clip reused inside a 12-cut window** (kills the C02-×11 problem).

## Beat sheet (lyric → image), aligned to the grid

**INTRO 0:00–0:18 (instrumental).** Rain sheeting down a dark 1970s window. The man
sits motionless, lit blue, an untouched glass, an empty chair across from him. Slow
push-in. On the last beat: one warm flash — her hand, gone before you register it.

**V1 0:18–0:41.**
- 0:19 *"I think I feel you"* — CU lead singing, eyes closed (present/blue).
- 0:23 *"soft touch upon my skin"* — MEMORY: her fingertips on his jaw, XCU, warm, no face.
- 0:31 *"heart had crossed the ocean"* — MEMORY: grey ocean meeting rain; her silhouette at the waterline, back to us.
- 0:35 *"rivers bend"* — a rain-swollen river bending through trees → match-cut to him at the glass.

**V2 0:41–1:10.**
- 0:43 *"look for you beyond my shoulder… between the drops"* — he turns to look behind him; rack-focus to drops on glass; a blurred figure between them.
- 0:55 *"the sky still holds your whisper… make my world stop"* — low cloud; the world literally stops: rain frozen mid-fall, him tiny inside it.

**PRE1 1:10–1:29 (build).**
- 1:10 *"don't know if you still feel it"* — him, present. 1:14 *"lose my breath"* — breath fogging cold glass.
- 1:18 *"trees are moving"* — trees thrashing, a door swinging. 1:26 *"never left"* — warm flash of her almost turning to face us; cut away before we see it. Band stirs.

**CHORUS 1 1:29–2:08 (release / storm peak).** Full band kicks in.
- 1:30 *"you're in the water"* — her form in the downpour / in a puddle reflection / the wet-street sheen.
- 1:42 *"like the thunder"* — lightning; on the flash, her face for a single frame, then gone.
- 1:53 instrumental swell — band + storm, wide and cathartic.

**V3 2:08–2:24 (fast, breathy).** *"think I hear you / wind howling / every storm a reminder / near but out of sight"* — quick present cuts: him listening, wind howling through a gap, storm out the window, him scanning an empty room on "out of sight."

**V4 2:24–2:53 (denial).**
- 2:24 *"hide your face in every mirror"* — he turns a mirror away; her face flickers in it anyway.
- 2:32 *"fold your memory inside a drawer"* — a hand closes a drawer on a photograph.
- 2:40 *"clouds would pass me"* / 2:44 *"like they always had before"* — he tells himself it'll pass; the storm doesn't.

**PRE2 2:53–3:34 (failed suppression, biggest build).**
- 2:52 *"trying to forget"* — jaw tight (performance). 3:03 *"see you in the sky"* — clouds resolve into her figure; he stares up into the rain.
- 3:15 *"still want you"* — he reaches a hand up into the downpour. 3:23 *"don't know why"* — storm to maximum; long build.

**CHORUS 2 3:34–3:58 (second release, bigger).** Same hook, fuller band + storm; she's everywhere in the water; lightning.

**BRIDGE 3:58–4:21 (climax / surrender).** He goes OUT into the rain, finally soaked.
- 3:58 *"where you landed"* — rain hitting water in extreme slow-mo. 4:02 *"words you said"* — her whisper at his ear (warm memory).
- 4:10 *"sun won't shine if you're not mine"* — sky darkens, flood rising at his feet. 4:14 *"a flood I can't defend"* — water overtakes; he stops fighting. The gut-punch.

**FINAL 4:21–4:34 (haunt).** Hook ×2, quieter — him alone, storm easing, a last glimpse of her dissolving into water. 4:29 *"and I still wonder"* — hold on his face in the last rain; cut to black on the final drop. Unresolved.
