# WHEN IT RAINS — Story Treatment (narrative cut)

**Logline.** A man moves through the ordinary rhythm of a rainy city — his apartment,
a coffee shop, the street, the train — and *she* is everywhere he isn't looking: in
the reflection of a glass case, a rain-streaked bus window, the black surface of his
coffee. She's gone. He keeps almost seeing her. The song is one day of grief carried
through normal life, until it floods.

**The rule (fixes "on the nose").** We do NOT illustrate the lyric literally. She
appears **obliquely — in reflections, glass, water, the edge of frame** — never
directly addressed. The rain is *weather and mood*, not a metaphor we point at. The
audience feels it before they understand it.

**Two layers, woven:**
- **STORY (most of the film):** him in grounded, real places; her as a ghost in
  reflections and glimpses. Naturalistic, handheld, desaturated 2000s grade.
- **PERFORMANCE (accents, mostly the choruses):** the hero rain-performance shots we
  already have — him singing — used sparingly as the emotional pulse, not the spine.

## Beat sheet (per section, tied to the lyric)

**INTRO (0:00–0:18, instrumental).** His apartment, grey rain light. He's still in
bed, awake, staring. The other half of the bed is empty and made. On the counter:
two mugs, one never used. He goes to the rain-streaked window — for a frame, her
outline is in the glass; he shifts, it's just the street. Establish: ordinary life,
her absence, the reflection motif.

**V1 (0:18–0:41) — "I think I feel you / soft touch / heart crossed the ocean / rivers bend."**
Morning routine, alone. Coffee, steam, rain on the window. On "soft touch," he pauses
as if a hand grazed his shoulder — turns, no one. A photo on the shelf: the two of
them by grey water (the "ocean"). He pockets his keys, heads out.

**V2 (0:41–1:10) — "I still look for you beyond my shoulder, in the space between the drops / the sky still holds your whisper, make my world stop."**
**THE COFFEE SHOP.** He's at the counter, rain outside. He glances up — and in the
**reflection of the glass pastry case**, *she's* there behind him, looking at him. He
turns fully — just other customers, the door, the rain. On "make my world stop," a
held beat: the room goes still around him while he stares at the empty space.

**PRE1 (1:10–1:29) — "don't know if you still feel it / lose my breath / the trees are moving / never left."**
Out on the wet street, umbrellas, wind in the trees. He sees her across the road; a
bus passes between them and she's gone with it — her face sliding away in the bus
windows. His breath fogs. She's everywhere in the city and nowhere.

**CH1 (1:29–2:08) — chorus.** PERFORMANCE pulse: the hero rain-performance (him
singing) intercut with her in the wet world — her reflection in a puddle he steps
around, in a shop window, in the rain on glass. Emotional lift, not literal.

**V3 (2:08–2:24) — "think I hear you / wind howling / every storm a reminder / near but out of sight."**
Home again, or a doorway. He hears something — turns fast. A curtain moving, a door
ajar. A glimpse of her at the end of a hall, gone before it resolves. "Near but out
of sight."

**V4 (2:24–2:53) — "tried to hide your face in every mirror / fold your memory inside a drawer / told myself the clouds would pass / like they always had before."**
**THE SUPPRESSION.** He catches her in the bathroom mirror — and turns it to the wall
/ drapes a towel over it. He gathers her things — a photo, a scarf — and folds them
into a drawer, slides it shut. He tells himself it's over. It isn't.

**CH2 (3:34–3:58) — chorus.** Bigger. The rain harder. Her in every reflective surface
now — the train window, the puddles, the wet street sheen — as he moves, faster,
trying to outrun it.

**PRE2 (2:53–3:34) — "keep trying to forget / but I see you in the sky / still want you / don't know why."**
He tries to be normal — the train, a bar, people — but a stranger turns and for a
second it's her; the sky in a window is her shape. The build tightens. He gives up
pretending.

**BRIDGE (3:58–4:21) — "every drop knows where you landed / every cloud holds the words you said / the sun won't shine if you're not mine / you're a flood I can't defend."**
**THE BREAK.** He goes back to *their* place — the grey waterfront from the photo — in
the pouring rain, soaked, no umbrella. This is where the performance-surrender lives:
he stops fighting it, the flood rising at his feet. The one moment he lets it take him.

**CH3 / CH4 / OUTRO (4:21–4:34) — chorus ×2 + "and I still wonder."**
Back to ordinary. The apartment, the coffee shop — calmer, emptied out. A last
reflection of her in the window; he almost smiles; she's gone. On "and I still
wonder," he looks out at the rain, still carrying it. Cut to black on a single drop.
